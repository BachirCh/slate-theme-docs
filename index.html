01

Overview

Assay is built for stores that sell formulated products such as skincare, hair care, supplements or cleaning products, where the numbers matter: the key active, its concentration and its pH. It works just as well with products that have no such data; cards then show a symbol made from the product name.

Six systems give the theme its structure. They are part of the layout, not colour or font choices:

SystemWhat it is
Ruled sheetEach section is a bordered frame on a backdrop. Content tiles edge to edge in cells separated by 1 px lines. Optional measuring rulers run down both sides of the sheet on wide screens.
Split headerA top row holds the logo, a search field, account and cart. A second row holds the menu as chips, plus a status message. Search opens as a command palette.
Spec cardsProduct cards laid out like an element in a table: position number, type and pH across the top, the concentration as the big figure, then the image, name, price and a plus-sign quick add cell.
Specimen mediaEvery image sits in a square grey cell with registration marks and a grid coordinate such as A1 or FIG. 01.
pH mapA chart in the home hero that plots every product in a collection on a pH scale, with a strip showing which part of the 0 to 14 range is magnified.
Regimen stepsA numbered, tabbed routine builder (morning and evening) that adds a whole routine to the cart.

The home page also includes a comparison table (Compare actives) and the product page has a Product comparison section that lines the product up against related products.

02

Install and first steps

  1. In Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes, add Assay to your library, and click Customize.
  2. Upload your logo under Theme settings → Logo and favicon. With no logo, the store name is shown as a text wordmark, set in the Text logo font.
  3. In the Header section, choose your menu. It uses main-menu by default. Set the Tag beside the logo (a small boxed label such as “Vol. 1”) or leave it empty.
  4. Create the product metafields described in Product metafields and fill them in on your products. Without them the theme still works; cards show a symbol instead of a concentration.
  5. On the home page, click each section and choose your own products, collections, blog and pages. Empty slots show placeholders until you do.
  6. Preview on mobile using the device toggle at the top of the editor, then click Save.
On a new install the home page keeps its structure (readout band, hero, formula table, comparison, regimen builder, skin type selector, journal and FAQ) but every product, collection, image and link slot is empty and shows a neutral placeholder.

03

Styles and colours

The theme ships one style, Assay: white sheet on a light grey backdrop, near-black text, a single blue signal colour, square corners and hairline grid lines. Change it under Theme settings → Colors:

GroupSettings
SheetSheet background, backdrop outside the sheet, text, secondary text, grid lines (keep at least 3:1 contrast with the sheet)
Specimen cellsImage cell background, panel background (readout bands and table headers)
SignalSignal colour and the text colour on it. Used for active states, pH chips and highlights.
ButtonsPrimary and secondary button and text colours
StatusSale price, in stock, rating
TagsSale, New, Selling fast and Sold out tag colours

The Stats band has its own three looks (Sheet, Panel, Ink). Shapes are square by default; add rounding under Shapes. The default colours meet WCAG AA contrast; check contrast again if you change them.

04

Header and navigation

On desktop the header has two rows inside the sheet frame. The top row holds the logo (with the optional boxed tag), a search field that shows the Ctrl/⌘ + K shortcut, an optional readout text, the account link (when customer accounts are on) and the cart with its count. The second row is the category bar: your menu as chips and a status message on the right.

SettingWhat it does
MenuThe menu shown in the category bar and the mobile sheet.
Sticky headerKeeps the header at the top while scrolling.
Tag beside the logoA short boxed label next to the logo.
Readout beside the search fieldA short line of text on wide screens.
LabelThe text at the start of the category bar (default “Browse”).
Show product counts on collection linksAdds a small two-digit count to chips that link to a collection.
Show collection images in menu panelsMenu items with sub-links open a panel of cells. Each cell shows the linked collection's image (or the linked product's image), its product count and third-level links.
Status messageA short line with a pulsing dot on the right of the category bar, for example a shipping note.

Search palette

The search field opens a palette that fills with live results as the customer types: products (with concentration, key active, pH and optional price), search suggestions, collections, pages and articles. You can add a Suggested links menu, shown before typing, and change the placeholder text under Search. Escape closes it and returns focus to where it was opened.

On mobile

The top row becomes logo, cart and menu button, with the search field on its own row below. The menu opens as a bottom sheet of numbered cells; items with sub-links expand in place.

05

Spec cards

Every product grid uses the same card, in the same ruled cell grid. From top to bottom:

  1. A head with the position number and product type on the left, and a pH chip with a small 0 to 14 scale on the right.
  2. The concentration as the large figure, with the key active underneath. If a product has no concentration, a two-letter symbol made from its name is shown instead.
  3. A square specimen cell with the product image, a grid coordinate (A1, B2…) and tags such as sale percentage, New and Selling fast.
  4. The name and price, and a plus-sign cell in the corner: it adds single-variant products straight to the cart, opens quick view for products with options, and turns into a disabled minus sign when sold out.

Set what appears under Theme settings → Spec cards and Specimen images:

SettingOptions
Metafield namespaceWhere the card reads concentration, key active and pH. Default custom.
Show position number / product type / pH chipTurn each part of the head on or off.
Show ratingReads the reviews.rating metafield that review apps write. Off by default.
Show second image on hoverSwaps to the second product image on hover.
Show quick add cellShows or hides the plus-sign cell.
Sale as percentageShows “-25%” on the tag instead of “Sale”.
Product tag for New / Selling fastProducts carrying these tags (defaults new and selling-fast) get the matching tag.
Image fitFit shows the whole product; Fill crops to the square.
Registration marks / grid coordinatesThe corner marks and labels on every image cell.

06

Product metafields

Spec cards, the pH map, the spec table, the comparison tables and the stats band read these product metafields. Create them under Settings → Custom data → Products as single-line text, with the namespace set in Spec cards → Metafield namespace (default custom, so the full key is custom.ph and so on):

KeyExampleUsed for
concentration10%The big figure on cards; comparison tables; spec table
key_ingredientNiacinamideCard, spec table, tables, regimen, cart line
ph5.5pH chip, pH map, spec table scale, stats, collection readout. Use a plain number.
skin_typeOily, combinationSpec table, comparison; matched by the Skin type selector
textureWater serumSpec table, comparison, product gallery caption

Rows with no value are hidden. The theme also reads the standard reviews.rating metafield for ratings.

07

Home page sections

The home page is built from these sections; add, remove and reorder them freely. Sections are numbered (“§ 03”) automatically in the order they appear.

SectionWhat it does and its settings
Stats bandA row of readouts. Settings: label, collection to measure, style (Sheet, Panel, Ink). Blocks (up to 6): Stat with a value source (custom text, number of products, pH range, average rating, number of key actives) and a label. Figures read the chosen collection; a stat with no data shows a dash.
Spec heroEyebrow, large heading, text, two buttons, a featured product with its spec table and quick add, and the pH map. Settings include Use as the page's main heading, the label above the product, an image reference label, Show pH map, the collection to plot and where the magnified range starts (pH 0 to 11; it spans 3 pH units).
Formula gridA grid of spec cards from a collection. Settings: heading, subheading, collection, maximum products (2 to 24), columns (3 to 6), link label, and Show the key cell with its name. The key cell is the first tile, annotating each part of a card.
Compare activesA sortable table of a collection or of products you pick: formula, key active, concentration, pH, skin type, texture, price, and a quick add or options button. Settings: heading, subheading, collection or products, maximum rows (2 to 24), footnote, link.
Regimen builderNumbered steps in morning and evening tabs. Blocks (up to 10): Step with routine (morning, evening or both), step name, product, dose, timing and an instruction. Shows the routine total and an Add routine to cart button (setting: Show add routine to cart button). Tab labels are editable.
Skin type selectorChips for skin profiles. Each Skin profile block (up to 6) has a label, match words, a description, a target pH and “look for” text. Products in the chosen collection whose skin_type metafield contains a match word are shown as cards (up to 12 per profile).
Journal gridLatest articles from a blog: heading, subheading, blog, number of posts (2 to 6), columns (2 to 4), excerpt on or off, link label.
FAQ tableQuestions and answers in two columns. Blocks (up to 40): Group heading and Question. Settings: heading, subheading, link, Use as the page's main heading, and Add FAQ structured data for search engines.
Claims gridUp to 8 Claim blocks, each with a figure, title, text and optional icon (molecule, leaf, shield, earth, sparkle, truck, return, box, clock). Also used on the product and About pages.
Specimen with textAn image next to text, with a caption label, a heading, text and a button, plus up to 8 Spec row blocks (label and value). Settings: image first, image label and reference, spacing.
Product comparison, Related formulasProduct page sections; see Product page.

08

Product page

The product page is a spec sheet: a two-column media grid on the left and a specification column on the right. The specification column can stay in view while the customer scrolls (setting: Keep the spec column in view while scrolling). On mobile the images become a swipeable strip with dots. Click an image to open it full screen. Images, videos, external videos and 3D models are supported. With grid coordinates on, each image is labelled FIG. 01, FIG. 02… On desktop the media grid stays in view beside the specification column.

The right column is made of blocks that you can reorder, remove and repeat where allowed: Text (custom text, vendor or product type), Title, Rating, Tags, Price, Spec table, Description (with optional word limit), Stock status, Variant picker, Buy buttons, Perks, Pickup availability, SKU, Tab, Share, Custom Liquid and app blocks.

BlockSettings
Spec tableHeading, Show pH scale and Show SKU row. Rows: concentration, key active, pH, skin type, texture (from metafields).
Stock statusShows the quantity in stock (optional) and a low-stock message under a threshold you choose (1 to 50).
Buy buttonsQuantity selector and dynamic checkout button can each be turned off.
PerksUp to three icon and text pairs in one row (box, truck, dollar, return, shield, leaf, gift).
TabConsecutive tab blocks form one tab set at the position of the first. Each has a heading and either rich text, the product description, or the content of a page you choose.
ShareShare link, and an optional “Ask a question” link.

Options appear as a row of buttons; options named Color, Colour or Shade also show a small swatch chip, using the swatch colour or image you set in Shopify (or the colour name). Unavailable values are struck through. Changing a variant updates the price, tags, stock, image and page address without reloading. Sold-out variants disable the add button. Below the spec sheet, the default template adds:

  • Product comparison: the current product and related products (from Shopify's Search & Discovery app) side by side, with the same columns as Compare actives. Settings: heading, subheading, readout, label for this product, number of related products (1 to 6), type (related or complementary) and a fallback collection.
  • Claims grid and FAQ table, which you can edit or remove.

You can also add Related formulas (product recommendations as spec cards) to the product, cart or any other page. Settings: heading, subheading, maximum products (2 to 10), columns (2 to 6), type and fallback collection.

09

Collections and search

The collection header shows the title and description beside a readout of the number of products, the pH range and the average rating. Below it, a toolbar holds the product count, filters, a grid/table switch and sort. Configure which filters appear in the Search & Discovery app.

SettingWhat it does
Enable filtering / sortingFilters appear as chips in the toolbar on desktop (price as a small popover) and open in a bottom sheet on mobile. Results update without reloading and the address keeps the choices. Active filters show as removable chips with a Clear all.
Default viewGrid of spec cards or Comparison table. Customers can switch; their choice is remembered in their browser.
Products per page (8 to 48)Pagination links update the results in place.
Columns on desktop (3 to 6), on mobile (1 or 2)Grid density.
Show collection descriptionShows the description under the title.

When no product matches, an empty state offers to clear the filters. The Collections page (Collections index) shows each collection as a cell with a two-letter symbol and its product count. Choose which collections appear, sort them (alphabetically, most or fewest products, newest, oldest), set columns (2 to 6) and choose whether empty collections are hidden.

Search results use the same cards and filters (results per page 8 to 48, columns 3 to 6) and a sort selector. With no results the page suggests checking the spelling. Search suggestions in the header palette can be turned off, or shown without prices, under Theme settings → Search.

Cart

The cart page is laid out as a report: a header with the date, number of lines, number of units and status, then a numbered table of lines with the key active, price, quantity and total. The drawer shows the same lines in a narrower form.

SettingWhat it does
Cart typeDrawer slides in after add to cart and from the header cart; Page goes to the cart page.
Free shipping thresholdDraws a progress meter toward free shipping. Set to 0 to hide it.
Gift wrap productCreate a product (for example “Gift wrap”) and select it. A checkbox on the cart page adds or removes it.
Enable order noteA collapsible note field on the cart page; it saves as the customer types.
Enable shipping estimatorCustomers enter a country, region and postal code to see shipping rates.
Show accelerated checkout buttonsIn the cart section settings.

An empty cart shows a message, a link to keep shopping and, if you set a fallback collection in the Related formulas section, suggested products.

12

Footer

The footer is a row of numbered cells (up to 8 blocks): Text (heading, text, optional link), Menu (any menu, with collection counts) and Newsletter (one only; subscribers are saved as customers tagged newsletter). Each block has a width. Below the cells, a strip holds the country/currency selector, the language selector, a policy menu, social links and payment icons; each can be turned off. Show large wordmark prints your store name (or text you enter) across the bottom, with a sheet note beside the copyright.

14

Languages and right-to-left

All text the theme prints comes from the locale file, so it can be translated in the editor (Online Store → Themes → … → Edit default theme content) or with Shopify's Translate & Adapt app. The theme ships English. When the storefront language is Arabic, Hebrew, Persian or Urdu, the page direction switches to right-to-left, layout properties are logical so the sheet mirrors, and directional arrows flip.

15

Performance and accessibility

  • No third-party scripts and no bundled libraries: one shared stylesheet and one JavaScript file, with component styles kept beside their markup and served by Shopify as a single compiled file.
  • Images use responsive sizes; the first image on a page loads eagerly with high priority and the rest load lazily. Body and heading fonts are preloaded.
  • Reveal-on-scroll only affects content below the first screen, and all motion is switched off for visitors who ask their device for reduced motion.
  • Skip link, one main heading per page, labelled form fields, focus rings, focus trapped inside open drawers and dialogs, Escape to close them, and focus returned to the control that opened them. Menu panels open on focus and with the down arrow key.
  • Colour defaults meet WCAG AA contrast; buttons and controls are at least 24 px.
